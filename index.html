Sizing and warehouse QC

Measure a garment you already wear

Lay it flat and measure across the chest, from shoulder to shoulder, and from shoulder to hem. Check whether the listing uses flat width or full circumference before comparing numbers.

Match photos to the exact variant

Compare the warehouse quality-control photos with your saved color and size. Request a close-up of any part that affects your decision, such as a size label, seam or zipper.

Check the exchange window

If a measurement or variant is wrong, review the seller and agent’s return terms while the item is still at the warehouse. Confirm any return shipping charge before deciding.

Compare two shipping quotes

Ask for the packed box dimensions and weight. Quote both lines for the same parcel, destination and currency. Check whether each line charges by actual weight or dimensional weight and whether the quotes include the same services.

KakoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is this an official KakoBuy catalog?

This is an independent shopping guide. The catalog rows come from CAFANS. Open the product listing to review available buying options.

What does the USD price include?

It is an estimated item price from CAFANS. Confirm the current product price with the selected agent and check domestic delivery, international shipping and any other charges before paying.

What if my size is missing or the product link changes?

Check the seller’s current listing for the exact variant. If it is unavailable, use the CAFANS catalog to find another listing and compare its size chart, price and return terms.
